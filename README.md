## 🛡️ Tournament Structure & Rest Protection System
To guarantee maximum competitiveness and avoid physical exhaustion, matches are staggered such that **no team ever plays two matches consecutively**:
* **Group Stage (Matches 1 to 6)**: Round-robin within each group.
* **Fluid Dynamic Qualification**:
  * The qualifier and semifinal fixtures are **100% fluid** and dynamically determined by how teams perform during the group matches (Points, Goal Difference, Goals Scored).
  * The **1st place** team in Group A and **1st place** team in Group B automatically advance directly into the **Semifinals**!
* **Playoff Qualifiers (Matches 7 & 8)**:
  * **Qualifier 1**: 2nd Group A vs 3rd Group B (determined by final Group A & B standings)
  * **Qualifier 2**: 2nd Group B vs 3rd Group A (determined by final Group A & B standings)
* **Semifinals (Matches 9 & 10)**:
  * **Semi-Final 1**: 1st Group A vs Winner Qualifier 2
  * **Semi-Final 2**: 1st Group B vs Winner Qualifier 1
* **Medal Matches**:
  * **3rd Place Playoff (Match 11)**: Runner-up SF1 vs Runner-up SF2
  * **Grand Final (Match 12)**: Winner SF1 vs Winner SF2

## 🚀 Key Website Features
1. **Player Roster Management**:
   * Pre-loaded starter squads with ability to add, edit, or delete player names, squad numbers, and positions (GK, DF, MF, FW, SUB).
2. **Real-Time Match Stats Console ("Side Drawer")**:
   * Accessible anywhere via the **⚡ Match Stats Logger** floating action button or clicking on any match fixture.
   * Log **Goals**, **Assists**, **Own Goals**, **Goalkeeper Saves**, **Yellow Cards**, and **Red Cards**.
   * Automatic recalculation of team scores from events.
   * Immediate live update of group tables, goal difference (GD), points, and tournament brackets!
3. **Interactive Knockout Bracket**:
   * Visual pathway with automatic advancement of winning teams.
4. **Leaderboards**:
   * **Golden Boot** (Top Goalscorers)
   * **Playmaker Award** (Most Assists)
   * **Golden Glove** (Goalkeeper Saves & Clean Sheets)
   * **Discipline & Fair Play** (Card tracker)
5. **Data Persistence & Backups**:
   * Automatically saved to browser LocalStorage.
   * 1-click JSON database backup and restore in the **Data & Backup** tab.
   * Printable summary sheet for referees and organizers.
